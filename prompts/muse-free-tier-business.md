# Prompts: recurring automations that fit the free tier

Context: Muse's free tier (~100M tokens/week) comfortably covers small recurring automations — Meta's own engineers reportedly used only 3% of a week's allowance during heavy use. These three prompts are starting points for building a zero-marginal-cost recurring service, not one-off tasks. Full writeup, including how to budget your quota before taking on clients: [Muse Free-Tier Business Ideas](https://aiextracash.com/tools/muse/muse-free-tier-business-ideas/).

**Note on quota**: tokens and referral bonuses are tied to your account and non-transferable — you can't resell capacity directly. What these prompts help you sell is your time plus Muse's output, at zero marginal AI cost to you.

## 1. Recurring digest

```
Every day at [time], check [source/topic] and send me a 5-bullet summary
via [WhatsApp/email]. Keep it short — headline plus one line of context
per item. Skip anything that hasn't changed since yesterday.
```

## 2. Light inbox triage

```
Check my inbox once a day at [time]. Flag anything urgent, draft short
replies to routine requests (don't send them, just draft), and give me
a one-line count of what you unsubscribed from or archived.
```

## 3. Batch classification

```
Here's a list of [items/rows]. Sort each one into one of these categories:
[category list]. Return the same list with a category column added, plus
a short summary of how many fell into each category.
```

## Before running any of these for a client

Run it on yourself first for a few days and check your usage gauge — that tells you the real per-client cost, not a guess. See the [quota-budgeting section](https://aiextracash.com/tools/muse/muse-free-tier-business-ideas/#budgeting-your-quota-like-a-business-not-a-hobby) of the source article for the full method.
